What Gotta Go is

Gotta Go is a free tool that helps you find public restrooms nearby. It is provided as-is, with no account and no subscription.

The information may be wrong

Restroom listings come from open community databases (OpenStreetMap and Refuge Restrooms) and from edits made by users. Nobody inspects these places. A restroom may be closed, locked, out of order, paid, or gone entirely, and hours and accessibility details may be out of date or simply incorrect.

Verify before you rely on it, especially for accessibility needs. We do not guarantee that any listing is accurate, current, available, safe, or suitable for you, and we are not responsible for what you find when you arrive.

Use it sensibly

Do not use the app while driving. Respect the rules of the businesses and public buildings you enter — a listing here is not permission to enter private property, and a place marked “free” may still have its own conditions. Follow local law.

Contributions

When you add or edit a listing, you are describing a public place. Submit information you believe to be true. Do not submit personal information about yourself or anyone else, harassment, advertising, or anything unlawful.

By contributing, you grant permission for that listing content to be shown in the app and, if you opt in, to be submitted to Refuge Restrooms under their terms. Contributed listing data is community data — you should expect others to be able to edit or correct it, just as you can edit theirs.

We may remove or correct any contribution that is inaccurate, abusive, or unlawful.

Ads and tips
